Headcanon: the desert world

This is a proof of concept for Headcanon's homepage, not a finished site. Nothing here is for sale and the shop behind it is not open.

Every fandom lives in the desert as a character in its own style. Hover a character to see whose it is, click the sand or the water, drag the clock to change the time, and scroll down to dive under the surface.
